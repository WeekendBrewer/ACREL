Methodology and interpretation

The experience measure is a proxy: ACREL join year minus the earliest recognized primary law-degree year in the education fields. It should not be described as exact years of legal practice because it does not account for bar-admission timing, career interruptions, non-practice periods, or foreign qualification pathways.

  • Recognized primary law degrees include J.D./JD, Juris Doctor variants, Doctor of Jurisprudence, L.L.B./LLB, and Bachelor of Laws.
  • Duplicate education entries are removed before selecting the earliest recognized primary law-degree year.
  • Verified source-year corrections are applied before calculation where a directory education year is known to contain a transcription error.
  • Implausible negative values or values above 70 years are excluded from the experience calculation.
  • The law-school view uses the institution tied to the recognized primary law degree. The college view uses the earliest recognized bachelor’s-degree institution. Obvious punctuation and abbreviation variants are consolidated where practical.
  • Expertise is multi-select. Expertise prevalence is the share of members in a cohort tagged with that expertise.
  • This dashboard describes historical membership patterns. It does not define or imply nomination criteria.
